# HTB writeups

Notes from boxes I've completed on Hack The Box. Starting Point boxes and
retired machines only. Anything still active stays off this account.

Each writeup follows the same shape: what the box is, what enumeration found,
what I tried that failed, how I got in, how I escalated, and what a defender
would have seen. The last section matters most to me.

## Why the writeups stop where they do

I do not publish walkthroughs for active machines. If a box is still running,
its writeup stays local until retirement, because the point of the lab is
solving it, not reading someone else's route through it.
